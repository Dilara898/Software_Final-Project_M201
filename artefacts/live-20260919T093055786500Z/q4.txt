Question: What is the current state of fusion energy research?

Current fusion energy research involves various national and international collaborative projects aiming to demonstrate its feasibility as a large-scale energy source [1, 2]. Major endeavors include the international ITER megaproject, which utilizes magnetic confinement in a tokamak reactor to achieve net-positive energy output, as well as research at NIF focused on maximizing energy yield [1]. Current efforts also focus on near-term R&D benchmarks and evaluating the investment landscape to determine fusion's viability for decarbonizing power systems [2]. While placing fusion energy on the electrical grid remains an ongoing effort, the research has already delivered real-world benefits by advancing plasma physics, fusion technology, materials, and industrial processes [3].
