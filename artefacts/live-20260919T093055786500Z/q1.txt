Question: What is photosynthesis and what are its main stages?

Photosynthesis is a vital process that transforms light energy into a usable form to support life on Earth [1]. It consists of two major stages: light-dependent reactions and light-independent reactions, which are also called the Calvin cycle [1, 2, 3]. In the light-dependent stage, which takes place in the thylakoid membrane, chlorophyll absorbs sunlight to convert light energy and water into oxygen, ATP, and NADPH [1, 2, 3]. During the light-independent stage, the plant uses the generated ATP and NADPH alongside carbon dioxide to capture and reduce carbon dioxide to create sugar [1, 3].
