Question: What is photosynthesis and what are its main stages?

Photosynthesis is a system of biological processes used by photopigment-bearing autotrophic organisms to convert light energy into the chemical energy needed to fuel their metabolism [1]. Typically referring to oxygenic photosynthesis, the process releases oxygen as a byproduct of water splitting and stores energy within intracellular organic compounds like carbohydrates [1]. The overall process can be broken down into two major stages: light-dependent reactions and light-independent reactions [8]. During the first stage, light-dependent reactions capture the energy of sunlight to generate the hydrogen carrier NADPH, ATP, and oxygen [7, 8, 9]. In the second stage, light-independent reactions use these products to capture and reduce carbon dioxide [7, 8].
