Question: What were the main causes of the 2008 financial crisis?

The 2008 financial crisis was primarily triggered by the collapse of the U.S. housing market and a banking crisis rooted in subprime mortgage lending [1, 2, 3]. The preceding housing boom was driven by loose credit, general exuberance, and excessive speculation on property values by both homeowners and financial institutions [1, 3].
