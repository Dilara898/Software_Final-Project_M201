Question: What is the current state of fusion energy research?

Nuclear fusion research currently involves various national and international collaborations, such as the ITER megaproject, which aims to demonstrate large-scale energy feasibility using magnetic confinement fusion in a tokamak reactor [1]. Key research goals include achieving sustained fusion reactions where energy output exceeds required input at ITER, alongside efforts at the National Ignition Facility (NIF) to maximize energy output [1]. Although research to put fusion energy on the power grid remains ongoing, it has already advanced plasma physics and fusion technology while delivering real-world benefits like improved materials and industrial processes [2]. However, despite recent publicity claiming commercial fusion could be achieved in the next 10 to 20 years, experts note that practical commercial energy from fusion remains an ongoing development effort [3].
